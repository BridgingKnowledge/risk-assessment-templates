# 🛡️ Risk Assessment Templates

This repository contains example templates and guidance for conducting risk assessments in regulated IT and QA environments.  
The goal is to make complex requirements **relatable, structured, and easy to communicate** across disciplines.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
- Provide clear and reusable templates for risk assessments
- Demonstrate how to document risks, controls, and residual risks
- Show how complex regulatory requirements can be translated into practical tools
- Support awareness and training in risk-based thinking

---

## 📂 Contents
- **FMEA_Template.md** – Example of Failure Mode and Effects Analysis for IT systems  
- **IT_Risk_Checklist.md** – Checklist for identifying and evaluating IT-related risks  
- **Risk_Evaluation_Example.md** – Example of a completed risk evaluation with controls and residual risk rating  

---

## 🧩 How to Use
1. Clone or download the templates.  
2. Adapt them to your own context (system, process, or project).  
3. Document risks, assign controls, and evaluate residual risk.  
4. Use the examples as inspiration for structuring your own assessments.  

---

## 🌍 Why This Matters
Risk assessments are not just about compliance – they are about **bridging knowledge**:  
- Helping technical experts, QA, and business stakeholders speak the same language  
- Making risks visible and manageable  
- Ensuring patient safety, data integrity, and regulatory compliance
